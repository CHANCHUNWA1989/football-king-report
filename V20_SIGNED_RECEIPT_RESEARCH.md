# V20 external receipt verification (HOLD/DISABLED)

`ops/v20_signed_receipts.audit_fixture` verifies one sealed BL1 forecast, one witness statement and two signed result receipts using OpenSSL Ed25519 and exact-byte domain separation. Trust policy JSON must come from an independent administrator and be pinned by a digest obtained separately. Each signer has key ID, organization, origin, role, public key hex and validity interval; result signers and witness require distinct declared organizations and origins. The signed witness timestamp must meet the existing archive audit's ten-minute creation window and precede kickoff by over ten minutes. Final receipts bind the same archive, native fixture/team IDs, kickoff, declared regulation final score and raw response digest. The supplied raw bytes must hash correctly; the actual provider-specific parsing and independent identity mapping are not implemented here. Distinct names or signatures alone do not prove organizations or upstream data are independent. The local `as_of_utc` is also not independent clock attestation.

No private keys, credentials, real signers, provider subscriptions or trust anchors are provisioned by this change. Synthetic tests create temporary Ed25519 keys and delete them after testing. Do not enroll production signers from this test. External custody, independent policy approval, trusted timestamping, provider-specific result parsing, source entitlements and independent reviewer/runtime attestation are required before any evidence credit. This is a library only, not a scheduled collector or live service. No odds or xG input; no automatic promotion. Verified forward sample credit remains zero.
